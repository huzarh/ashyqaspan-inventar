The original credit (ESP32-C3-DevKit-RUST-2 rev 2.0, Espressif Systems / esp-rs) is kept in both title blocks, on the
board's silkscreen and here. The unmodified upstream design files are available at that tag; in the Köpir git history
they are commit `825c90a` (the release zips carry no git history). The ESP32-C3 core, USB and power circuitry and the
routing of the main board are Espressif's design.

`kicad_libs/Espressif.pretty/ESP32-C3-MINI-1.kicad_mod` was exported from the upstream board. It has the pad geometry
of the ESP32-C3-MINI-1 footprint in Espressif's KiCad library (espressif/kicad-libraries, CC-BY-SA-4.0 with a design
exception); its origin (offset 2.7 mm) and the numbering of its GND pads (36) differ from the library version.

## Modifications (CERN-OHL-P-2.0 §3.3(b))

Modified by **Ashyq Aspan Foundation** (Kazakhstan), 2026-11-10 — **Köpir C3, rev A**:

1. Project renamed to `kopir-c3`; stale upstream output files removed (outputs are regenerated in `fab/`).
2. BOM refresh for assembly at JLCPCB (JLCPCB stock checked 2026-11-10):
   - U3 ESP32-C3-MINI-1-N4 (NRND) → **ESP32-C3-MINI-1-H4X** (LCSC C41349510; chip revision v1.1, same footprint)
   - U2 ICM-42670-P: LCSC **C3288646** added (was empty)
   - J1 USB-C: genuine **GCT USB4105-GF-A** (LCSC C3020560) instead of a third-party equivalent
   - D5/D7/D8 LESD5D5.0CT1G: LCSC **C5274290** (the previous number was nearly out of stock)
   - D9 WS2812B: upstream WS2812B-MINI-V3/W (C527089) is out of JLC stock → same-family **WS2812B-MINI-V6**
     (LCSC C52941386), identical land pattern and pinout, rated VDD 3.3–5.3 V (in spec on the 3.3 V rail). A
     WS2812B-MINI-V3J substitute was rejected: its LCSC land pattern is the mirror image of this footprint.
   - SW1/SW2: C&K PTS810SJM250 (C116501, nearly out of stock) → **PTS810SJG250SMTRLFS** (LCSC C221895), same
     family, land pattern and terminal pairing, firmer actuation
   - U1 SHTC3: **DNP** (not needed on a drone; footprint kept, silkscreen marks it "T+H opt.")
   - MPN / Manufacturer fields added; D9 and Q1 datasheet links corrected, SW1/SW2 datasheet link added
3. Rebranding (CERN-OHL-P §7.2). Removed: the esp-rs logo artwork (including the top-side copper logo), the "ESP-RS"
   mask text, the "Espressif Systems" label on the back and the "ESP32-C3" label beside the antenna, the
   "Rust Board 2.0", "ESP32-C3-DevKit-RUST-2 v2.0 10/2025" and "github.com/esp-rs" silkscreen texts, the "2544" date
   code, the esp-rs logo bitmap on the schematic and the two esp-rs logo footprints in the project library
   (`Rust_Board.pretty`). The F.Fab title table (company, title, revision, date) was rewritten for Köpir C3. Added:
   the Ashyq Aspan logo, the name **KÖPIR C3**, revision and date, a factual attribution line and the licence name
   (all at least 1.0 mm high), and title blocks that carry the original credit.
4. Design hygiene: PWR_FLAG symbols on the six connector-powered nets (ERC "power pin not driven"); D1 footprint type
   set to SMD; the ESP32-C3-MINI-1 and SOD-882 footprints, which only existed embedded in the board, exported to
   `kicad_libs/` and registered in the project footprint table; schematic LED note corrected (the red LED is on
   GPIO10); title block comments shortened; back silkscreen lines moved clear of the IO9/BOOT label.
5. **No track, pad or via changes on the main board.** Copper changed only through the removed top-side logo and the
   GND pours. These were refilled and now keep 0.3 mm from the routed edge and the mounting cut-outs and 0.25 mm from
   the unplated peg holes of the USB-C connector J1, as JLCPCB requires (zone-only rules in `kopir-c3.kicad_dru`).
   The bottom GND pour is now six regions tied together by vias. Verified against the upstream design with
   `scripts/check.py`: all 707 tracks/vias and 240 pads identical; identical connectivity (55 components, same nets);
   schematic parity clean; ERC 0 errors / 66 warnings (original: 6 errors / 67 warnings), DRC 0 errors / 52 warnings
   (original: 3 errors / 60 warnings).
6. JLCPCB placement file: every part's rotation and centre are solved against the LCSC part's own footprint, pads
   paired by pin function (`scripts/jlc_cpl_fix.py`): Q1 +180°, U5/U6 +270°, J1 centre 2.38 mm. The LEDs D2/D3 stay
   at 0° although their LCSC footprint numbers the anode as pad 1.
7. Drone I/O (JST-SH flight-controller port with fused and TVS-clamped 5 V input, Qwiic I²C port, AUX UART with
   fused 5 V) is provided by the separate **Köpir Wing** add-on board (`hardware/kopir-wing/`, generated by
   `scripts/make_wing.py`). It was designed by Ashyq Aspan Foundation and is also licensed under CERN-OHL-P-2.0.
